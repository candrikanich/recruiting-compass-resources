If you want to compete at a Division I or Division II school, the NCAA Eligibility Center is non-negotiable. It's the office that decides whether your grades, courses, and amateur status clear you to play. Here's how to register without the last-minute panic.

## When to Register

Register at the **start of junior year**. You don't need to wait until you've picked a school — coaches often ask for your NCAA ID before they'll talk seriously.

- **Junior year:** Create your account, enter your courses, request transcripts.
- **Senior year:** Confirm your final transcript and SAT/ACT scores are sent.

## What You'll Need

1. A valid email address and about 30 minutes.
2. Your high school's NCAA core-course list (your counselor has it).
3. The registration fee (fee waivers exist if you qualify for one on the SAT/ACT).

## The Core-Course Rule

The Eligibility Center only counts **16 approved core courses**. Electives don't move the needle. Map your schedule against your school's core-course list early — a class that "felt academic" may not count.

## Common Mistakes

- Waiting until senior spring to register, then missing a transcript deadline.
- Assuming every class counts toward the core GPA.
- Forgetting to mark amateurism questions before your first college competition.

## Next Step

Once registered, write down your **NCAA ID** and put it in your email signature to coaches. It signals you're serious and organized.
